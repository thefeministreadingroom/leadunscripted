Saturday by Saturday

Every session, and what it covers

Four Saturdays, 9:45 to 17:00, online, each ending with 15 minutes of questionnaire and feedback. You build as you learn, so by the last Saturday your business is running online.

Saturday 1

Foundations: your AI toolkit and your brand

Morning

AI literacy for business

  • How AI assistants work, in plain words, and what they are good and bad at
  • Writing clear instructions: describe, show an example, then improve
  • Checking what AI tells you: facts, numbers, tone and made-up details
  • Keeping your customers’ details and your passwords safe
  • Your own prompt library, started on the day
Afternoon

Your brand, built with AI

  • Your idea, your customer and your story in one page
  • Testing business names, and checking that the web address is free
  • Buying your domain without paying for add-ons you do not need
  • A simple logo, the small icon in the browser tab, your colours and fonts
  • Your tone of voice, and a one-page brand kit you use everywhere
Saturday 2

Build: your website and your business email

Morning

Your website, live on your own domain

  • Planning your pages: home, about, what you offer, prices, contact
  • Writing every page with AI, then making it sound like you
  • Designing the look with your brand, and adding your own photos
  • Making it work on phones, with clear menus and buttons
  • Putting it online and connecting your domain
Afternoon

Business email, forms and sign-ups

  • An email address on your domain, such as hello@, shared by partners if you like
  • The settings that keep your emails out of spam, explained like a postal address
  • Contact, order and registration forms that send details straight to you
  • A price list or a simple calculator that helps customers decide
  • Member or customer sign-up, with consent and privacy done properly
Saturday 3

Run: customers, money and automation

Morning

Your customer and money dashboard

  • One place for every registration, order, enquiry and follow-up
  • Tracking deals from first contact to paid
  • Payments received, payments owed, expenses, and profit or loss by month
  • Downloading your data for your accountant
  • A private admin page that only you and your partners can open
Afternoon

Automatic emails and routine tasks

  • Confirmations, welcome emails and reminders that send themselves
  • Birthday wishes, offers and vouchers for your members or customers
  • Letting AI handle a routine task on a schedule, and checking its work
  • Translating your pages and messages to reach people in other languages
  • Knowing what to automate and what should always stay personal
Saturday 4

Grow: marketing, partnerships and your launch

Morning

Social media, newsletters and events

  • A 30-day content calendar, planned in an hour
  • Carousels and posters in your brand, for LinkedIn and Instagram
  • Captions and hashtags that sound like you
  • Email announcements and newsletters to your community
  • Running online events, meetups and community programmes such as scholarships or giveaways
Afternoon

Pitch, partner and launch

  • A proposal with clear prices and what is included
  • A pitch deck in your brand, for clients, sponsors or investors
  • Approaching brands for collaborations and partnerships
  • Your launch-day checklist and your next 90 days
  • Showcase: each woman presents what she built

Questions

Before you join

Do I need any tech background?

No. If you can send a message on your phone, you can do this. You describe what you want in plain words, the AI does the technical part, and we show you how to check it.

What will it cost me to run, after the programme?

Very little, and you pay providers directly, never us. A domain costs about ₹1,000 (US$10 to 11) a year. Hosting and business email have free plans to start with, and paid plans if you need more. In each session you can set things up live, or learn the steps and do them later with our checklist.

What if I fall behind or get stuck?

Between Saturdays there is a short task list and help in the group chat, every topic has a user guide, and there is a free check-in 30 days after the last Saturday.

What do I need?

A laptop, an internet connection, your phone, and some photos of what you make or offer. Bring your idea, or the business you already run.

Is this only for new businesses?

No. Many women come with a business that already sells through WhatsApp or Instagram, and want it to look and run professionally.
