Our method

Leadership happens in conversations, so that is where we train it

We use theatre-based practice: managers rehearse the real moments of their week, get feedback, and try again, until the new way feels natural. Fewer slides, more practice, and groups we advise keeping under 30.

01

Diagnose

Interviews and an anonymous team pulse show what is really happening.

02

Design

Scenarios written from your managers’ real situations.

03

Rehearse

Hard conversations practised safely, with actors’ techniques.

04

Embed

Handover to business as usual, with simple habits to keep.

05

Measure

Checks at 30, 60 and 90 days, and retraining where needed.

For HR and L&D leaders

Seven questions to ask any training provider

We don’t make you wait for the answers. Click each question to see right away how we deliver on it.

  1. Who exactly will deliver our programme, and can we meet them before we sign?

    Shilpi, Christina (pro bono), or a vetted facilitator from our network, named in the proposal. You meet them before you sign, Your programme is designed by our content team and delivered by that named trainer, who is fully briefed on it before the day.

  2. How much of our fee does that facilitator receive?

    Most of it. There is no intermediary between us, so your fee pays for the expertise in the room, not for an introduction. That is also why our day rate is lower.

  3. Did you design this programme, or is it bought in?

    We design every programme ourselves, after a diagnostic with your managers and teams, using scenarios from their real working week.

  4. How will you find out what our managers need before training?

    A short diagnostic before any training: conversations with HR and a sample of managers, plus a 10-minute anonymous team survey. If you already run a pulse survey, we start from your report.

  5. How will we know behaviour changed, and when will you measure it?

    An anonymous team survey scored against a rubric built for you: a baseline before training, then again at 30, 60 or 90 days, depending on your plan.

  6. What happens after the training day?

    We stay. Free follow-up at 30, 60 or 90 days, and a free refresher for anyone whose team scores have not moved.

  7. Will you commit to results in writing?

    Yes. Scope, follow-up dates, what we measure and the free refresher are all in the contract, and you receive a written report at the end.
