AI literacy Saturdays for women

A free series, every Saturday, for women who run or are starting a small business and need help getting online. It is a non-profit initiative: we want these places to reach the women who need them most, so you apply for each session and tell us about your business and why you need it.

10 places per session, so everyone gets help
Every Saturday, 11:00 am to 1:00 pm IST, online
Apply separately for each session you want to join
We review every application and confirm by email
Completely free. No payment details, ever

Not the same as our paid workshop. These short free sessions each cover one topic. Our paid one-day workshop, Launch your business with AI, in a day, is a full Saturday where you build and launch your business online, step by step.

Every Saturday, November and December

Interview prep workshops for women

Have an interview coming up with Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Accenture or Deloitte? Each workshop focuses on one company: how it interviews, the answer method it expects, and question templates you fill with your own experience. Then you practise out loud and get feedback. The November workshops are a free pilot.
