Your questions

Before you book

Do we sign anything with the trainer?

No. You have one contract with Unscripted, our Client Terms of Business, and a one-page order form for each engagement. We contract, pay and manage the trainer.

Who invoices us?

Only Unscripted, with one invoice per engagement that carries your PO number and the attendance record, so it can be approved first time. The trainer never invoices you.

What if the trainer cannot make it?

We send a replacement trainer who has completed the same train-the-trainer. If we cannot, we move the session to a date that suits you, at no extra cost.

Can we hire the trainer directly afterwards?

Our trainers are vetted, briefed and managed by us, so for 12 months after an engagement we ask you to book them through Unscripted. If you would like to hire one directly, talk to us and we will agree a fair introduction fee.

Do you charge for travel?

Only when your order form says so. Most engagements use a trainer local to your team, so there is no travel at all.

How do you handle our people’s data?

We use participant details only to run and follow up the training, report feedback to you in summary rather than by name, and delete the data after the follow-up period, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
