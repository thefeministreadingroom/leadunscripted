Keep trust and momentum while restructures, mergers or new ways of working land.

What changes

By the end of the day, participants can

  • Explain the why behind a change in a way people accept
  • Respond to resistance with curiosity, not force
  • Support people through uncertainty and loss
  • Keep the team performing during transition

How we use theatre

Techniques from the rehearsal room

People learn leadership the way actors learn a part: by doing it, seeing it from the other side, and doing it again.

Image theatre

Teams build still images of “before”, “during” and “after” the change, which surface fears that words miss.

Hot-seating

Leaders face the questions their teams are really asking, from a facilitator in role.

Rehearsal for reality

Managers rehearse their actual change announcement and one-to-one follow-ups.
