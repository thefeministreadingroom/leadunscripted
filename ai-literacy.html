Why now

The tools have changed. The gap has not closed yet.

The work has changed

Websites, branding, marketing and admin used to need an agency or a tech team. Now they need clear instructions and good judgement.

The cost has collapsed

A domain costs about ₹1,000 (US$10 to 11) a year, and hosting and email have free plans to start with. The barrier is knowing how, not money.

The gap is real

25% lower

Women adopt AI tools at a 25% lower rate than men, on average, across 18 studies of more than 140,000 students and workers worldwide. Every month that gap stays open, it grows. We are here to close it.

Source: Harvard Business School, “Global Evidence on Gender Gaps and Generative AI”, Koning, Otis, Delecourt and Cranney, 2025

Your day, step by step

From idea to launch-ready

The whole day is a working session. We guide, you build, and an ideation workshop runs through it so your decisions fit together.

01

Ideate

Who you serve, what you sell, what makes you different, and the story behind it.

02

Name it

Choose a business name and check and pick a domain name, the web address that is yours.

03

Design

Build your website with AI: the pages, the words, the look, and photos of what you sell.

04

Host it

Put the site online, connect your domain, and set up an email address on it.

05

Prepare to launch

Prices, an order or contact form, payment basics, and a launch checklist.

06

Market it

A social media plan with AI-assisted posts, captions and visuals for your first month.
