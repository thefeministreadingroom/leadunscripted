Privacy

Privacy notice

How Unscripted collects, uses and protects your personal data. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Who we are

Unscripted is a women-led leadership and learning studio founded by Shilpi Khanna and Christina Shikha. For any privacy question or complaint, email hello@leadunscripted.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, phone number and city, and your answers, such as details of your business, the company you are interviewing with, or the training your team needs.

Why we use it

To review your registration or application, to contact you about the session or service you asked about, and to answer your questions. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for anyone else’s marketing.

Your consent

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you submit a form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we will stop using your data and delete it.

Who can see it

Only the Unscripted founders and the team members who handle your request. Your data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, and our email provider, Zoho.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after your last contact with us, or less if you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can raise a grievance with us. Email hello@leadunscripted.com and we will reply within 7 working days.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you are a team member who signs in to the admin page, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
